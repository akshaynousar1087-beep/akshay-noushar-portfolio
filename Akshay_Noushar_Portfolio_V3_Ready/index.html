Akshay Noushar is an artist, visual storyteller and creative director working across writing, photography, cinematography, direction and post-production.

His journey developed through independent filmmaking, wedding and event work, commercial productions and hands-on production experience. From screenplay and camera to lighting, direction and edit, he often works across the complete visual process.

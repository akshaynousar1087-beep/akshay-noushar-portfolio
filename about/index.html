Akshay Noushar is an artist, visual storyteller and creative director working across writing, photography, cinematography, direction and post-production.

THE BEGINNING

Cinema was the reason.
The route became the career.

I started shooting weddings to make enough money to make a short film.

That practical decision became years of hands-on production experience — working with people, cameras, light, time, crews, pressure and real deliverables — while the ambition to make cinema stayed underneath it all.
